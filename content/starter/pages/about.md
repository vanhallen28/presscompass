PressCompass is an independent, English-language news site about Indonesia, the world's fourth most populous country and one of its largest economies. We write for readers who follow Indonesia from inside or outside the country and want the facts, the context and the numbers in one place.

## What we publish

- **News** on what is happening and why it matters.
- **Explainers** that set out how institutions, markets and policies work.
- **Data stories** built on official statistics, with charts and the figures behind them.
- **Analysis and opinion**, always labelled as such.

## How we work

Every article lists its sources at the end, with the date we checked them. Figures come with their date and their origin. When we get something wrong, we correct it and add a dated note to the article saying what changed.

We do not take sides in elections or between parties. Opinion pieces are labelled and reflect the views of their authors.

## Advertising

PressCompass may show advertising to pay for its work. Advertisers have no say over what we write, and ads are always labelled "Advertisement". See our privacy policy for how ads use cookies.
