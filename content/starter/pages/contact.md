We read every message. Tell us what you think, point out a mistake, or send us a story we should look into.

## Email

Write to **[add your contact email here]**. Please put one of these words at the start of the subject line so your message reaches the right person:

- **Correction:** for a factual error in an article. Include the link to the article and what you think is wrong.
- **Tip:** for a story idea or information you want to share.
- **Advertising:** for advertising and partnership questions.
- **Privacy:** for questions about your data or requests under our privacy policy.

## Corrections

When we correct an article, we add a dated note to it that says what changed. We do not quietly change facts.
