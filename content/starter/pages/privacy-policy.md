This policy explains what information is collected when you read presscompass.site, who collects it, why, and the choices you have. It covers readers of the public site.

**Who is responsible:** PressCompass, presscompass.site. Contact: **[add your contact email here]**.

## What we collect

You do not need an account to read PressCompass, and we do not ask for your name or email address.

- **Technical data needed to run the site.** When you open a page, our hosting provider (Vercel) and our image provider (Cloudinary) receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, to deliver the page and keep it secure.
- **Your choices on this site.** Your cookie choice is stored in a cookie named pc_consent for 12 months. If you choose a light or dark theme, that choice is stored in your browser's local storage. Both stay on your device and are needed for the site to remember your choices.

## Advertising and measurement

PressCompass may show advertising from Google (Google AdSense and Google Ad Manager) and other advertising networks, and may use the Meta Pixel to measure visits from Meta's advertising.

These services only run if you choose **Accept all** in the cookie banner. With **Necessary only**, no advertising or measurement code from Google, Meta or other networks is loaded.

If you accept, these partners may use cookies and similar technology on your device to show ads, to limit how often you see them, to measure them, and to personalise ads based on your activity on this and other sites. They act under their own privacy policies:

- Google: [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). You can manage personalised ads at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com).
- Meta: [Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy).

Readers in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland may also be asked for their choices about Google ads in a message from Google.

## Changing your choice

Use **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page to change your choice at any time. If you withdraw your consent, we remove the advertising cookies set on our domain and reload the page so the advertising code stops.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP) and the EU and UK GDPR, you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, to correct or delete it, and to withdraw your consent. Email us at the address above. For data held by Google, Meta and other partners, use the tools in their policies.

## How long data is kept

The pc_consent cookie lasts 12 months. Data kept by our hosting, image and advertising partners is kept for the periods set out in their own policies.

## Changes

We will update this page when our use of data changes, and show the date of the last update at the bottom of the page.
