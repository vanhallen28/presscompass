For most of Indonesia's history, the president was chosen by the People's Consultative Assembly. Amendments to the Constitution after the fall of Suharto changed that, and since 2004 voters have elected the president and vice-president directly, as a pair on one ticket.

## The rules

- **Term:** five years. A president may serve two terms.
- **Winning in the first round:** a pair needs more than half of all votes, and at least 20 per cent of the vote in more than half of the provinces.
- **Runoff:** if no pair meets both conditions, the two pairs with the most votes face each other in a second round, and the one with more votes wins.

The General Elections Commission (KPU) runs the vote and announces the official result. Challenges go to the Constitutional Court.

## The 2024 result

The last election was held on 14 February 2024, on the same day as the parliamentary and regional legislative elections. On 20 March 2024, the KPU declared Prabowo Subianto and Gibran Rakabuming Raka the winners in the first round, with 58.59 per cent of the 164.2 million valid votes. They won in 36 of the 38 provinces, and turnout was 81.78 per cent.

The two other pairs, Anies Baswedan with Muhaimin Iskandar, and Ganjar Pranowo with Mahfud MD, challenged the result at the Constitutional Court, which rejected their cases. Prabowo and Gibran were sworn in on 20 October 2024.

## Who can stand next time

Until recently, only parties or coalitions holding at least 20 per cent of the seats in the House of Representatives, or 25 per cent of the national vote, could nominate a presidential pair. That rule, in Article 222 of the 2017 Election Law, kept the number of candidates small: there were only two pairs in 2014 and 2019, and three in 2024.

On 2 January 2025, the Constitutional Court struck the threshold down in decision 62/PUU-XXII/2024. Justice Saldi Isra said the threshold, "regardless of its size or percentage", was contrary to the Constitution. Lawmakers are expected to set new nomination rules in a revised election law before the next presidential election, due in 2029.
